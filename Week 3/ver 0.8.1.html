NODE MUTATION

Select Feed on a fish or bird to increase its health value. Vaccinate poultry to boost health and reduce its next daybreak loss. Health is stored in the BST node; it is not part of the ordering key, so changing it mutates node data while preserving the search-tree ordering. Harvest removes a mature node, then AVL rotations restore balance.

Field Notebook · Python Engine

The farm runs on Python data structures. Each engine section is commented with its structure type. Peek inside, experiment, then run your version.

Engine starts automatically when the game opens. Running changed code restarts the farm and replaces its current save.

GARDEN ALGORITHMS

OOP: Plant, ResourceBag, and animal classes model game objects and their data.

Stack: farm chores save snapshots; Undo pops and restores the latest one.

Queue: the oldest climate forecast is removed first; a new forecast joins the back.

2D Grid: the 5×5 farm stores each Plant object in a row-and-column cell.

Hierarchical Trees: watering, harvesting, and daybreak run through parent/child action nodes.

Tree Traversals: BFS (level-order); DFS (preorder for pest detection, inorder for sorted BST visits, postorder for cleanup).

Binary Search: fish and poultry BSTs compare keys and search left or right; AVL rotations keep the trees balanced.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose Eggplant, Apples, Banana, Melon, or Pineapple and plant an empty bed.
  2. Water it, then wait for daybreak.
  3. Harvest mature crops into your basket.
  4. Complete village orders for coins and XP.
  5. Buy supplies, tend pests, and grow your farm reputation.

A day ends every 2 minutes. You can also tap Next day to keep the garden moving.
