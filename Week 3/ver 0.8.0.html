The farm runs on Python data structures. Peek inside, experiment, then run your version.

Engine starts automatically when the game opens. Running changed code restarts the farm and replaces its current save.

GARDEN ALGORITHMS

Resource bag: seed, water, produce, spray, and coins are held in a Python inventory object.

Action stack: farm chores save snapshots; Undo pops and restores the latest one.

Climate queue: the oldest forecast becomes today's weather, then a fresh forecast joins the back.

Action trees: watering, harvesting, and daybreak follow check-then-act hierarchies.

Tree traversals: pest scan uses preorder, the sweep visits level-order, cleanup uses postorder.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Pick a crop and Plant an empty bed.
  2. Water it, then wait for daybreak.
  3. Harvest mature crops into your basket.
  4. Complete village orders for coins and XP.
  5. Buy supplies, tend pests, and grow your farm reputation.

A day ends every 2 minutes. You can also tap Next day to keep the garden moving.
