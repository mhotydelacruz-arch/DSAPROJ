POND AVL TREE · IN-ORDER SHAPE
Fingerling → Juvenile → Market-size. Fish age at daybreak. Feed raises health; harvesting a market-size fish removes its node and AVL rotations keep the pond tree balanced.

🐔 Clover Coop

A second balanced BST keyed by age in days. Feeding and vaccination update each bird's health value without changing its key.

🐔 Flock: 0 / 8
Key: (age in days, bird ID)
COOP AVL TREE · IN-ORDER SHAPE
Chick → Pullet → Layer. Layers lay eggs at daybreak. Harvesting a layer yields poultry and deletes its node; each deletion rebalances the tree.

NODE MUTATION

Select Feed on a fish or bird to increase its health value. Vaccinate poultry to boost health and reduce its next daybreak loss. Health is stored in the BST node; it is not part of the ordering key, so changing it mutates node data while preserving the search-tree ordering. Harvest removes a mature node, then AVL rotations restore balance.

📜 Project Journal · How the algorithms fit

Inventory: Insertion Sort is a good fit for a small harvested basket; it can rank crop objects by weight, current value, or days to grow.

Leaderboard: Merge Sort ranks seasonal scores in O(n log n) time and is stable, so equal scores preserve their previous order. Quick Sort is used for a fast re-rank when crash-market prices change.

Plant table: a coordinate such as (2, 3) hashes to a bucket. The stored value contains plant stats. Separate chaining handles collisions; rehashing allocates more buckets and re-indexes entries once the load factor is too high.

# inventory is a list of plant dictionaries

HOW UNLOCKS WORK

  1. Plant, water, harvest, trade and tend the farm to earn Insight.
  2. Open this notebook and choose a topic once its Insight requirement is met.
  3. The topic unlocks its linked feature. Core farming remains playable from the start.
  4. Unlock progress is saved on this device.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose an unlocked crop and plant an empty bed.
  2. Draw water at the well, water planted crops, and wait for daybreak.
  3. Harvest ripe crops into the basket.
  4. Learn Queues & Deques to access village orders and the weather queue.
  5. Open Market & Algorithms to trade, sort and study your harvest.

A day ends every 2 minutes. Use Next day to advance sooner.
